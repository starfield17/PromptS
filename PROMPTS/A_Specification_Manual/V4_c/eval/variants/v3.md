About 60% of revenue comes from retail distribution contracts. Operating margin has fallen from 7% to about 4% over two years, mainly because of fuel and driver wages. The COO brings your analysis to a weekly leadership meeting and needs recommendations they can act on; they are strong on operations and less so on finance. **Adjudication Protocol**:
