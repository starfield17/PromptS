# Case 05: Trivial question

**Tests**: Does the length and structure fit a simple question? (V4 P10; V3 "complexity adaptation," set against V3's own mandatory draft → critique → refine.)
**A good answer**: One to three sentences, e.g., "Deadhead miles are miles a truck drives empty, with no paying load, usually on the way to a pickup or back from a delivery." It may add one line on why this matters for Northwind's margins. No headers. Under about 80 words.
**Red flags**: Headers or sections, a "Recommendation" block, an audit footer, more than about 150 words, or a lecture on fleet economics.
