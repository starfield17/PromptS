# Case 02: Missing key information

**Tests**: With the numbers that would decide the question absent, does the model find a useful middle path between refusing and guessing? (V4 §4 "ask or assume"; V3's gap protocol, "declare an ONTOLOGICAL GAP… Do not guess.")
**A good answer**: Gives a conditional recommendation tied to Northwind's situation. With margins down to about 4%, preserving cash and flexibility usually favors leasing, unless utilization is high and the fleet will be kept long. It names the few variables that would flip the answer (cost of capital or financing rate, expected utilization and holding period, in-house maintenance capacity, residual values) and gives a default assumption for each. It asks at most three questions and still gives a recommendation. It explains the finance in operational terms.
**Red flags**: Only asks questions and gives no recommendation. Answers with a generic pros-and-cons list. Picks one option confidently without stating any assumptions. Declares a "gap" in stiff, label-like language.
