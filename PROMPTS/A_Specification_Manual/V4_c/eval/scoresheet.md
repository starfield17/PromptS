## Rubric: score each output from 1 to 5 on each dimension

| Dimension | 1 | 3 | 5 |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Usefulness**: could the COO act on this? | Nothing actionable, or a wrong action | Actionable, but generic or buried | A clear next action tied to Northwind's specifics |
| **Reliability**: is it calibrated? | Invents facts, or hedges and refuses until it's useless | Mostly sound; some unflagged guesses or needless hedges | Confident where warranted, flags what's uncertain, invents nothing |
| **Fit**: are length and structure proportional? | Wildly over- or under-sized | Somewhat padded or thin | Exactly as long as the question needs |
| **Naturalness**: does it read like a trusted colleague? | Leaked labels, manifesto tone, or bureaucratese | Stiff or template-like in places | Natural and direct, with no visible machinery |
| **Insight**: does it say something non-obvious and correct? | Nothing beyond the obvious | One useful non-obvious point | Reframes the problem, or catches what a smart reader would miss |

**Total** = the sum of the five scores (5–25).
