# Luna OCR Reference

Luna can act as a low-cost visual extraction agent.

Workflow:

Image/document
    ↓
Luna OCR xhigh
    ↓
Structured transcription
    ↓
Terra synthesis (if complex)
    ↓
Sol decision

Rules:
- preserve original structure;
- mark uncertainty;
- do not hallucinate missing characters;
- separate transcription from interpretation.
