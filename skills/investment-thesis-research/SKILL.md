# Investment Thesis Research

Use this skill to convert a direction, trigger, sector theme, company narrative, or market event into a structured buy-side research memo.

## Core behavior

- Start from the trigger and why it matters now.
- Separate facts, market consensus, and possible non-consensus insight.
- Think in value chains, profit pools, bottlenecks, bargaining power, and competitive intensity.
- Identify who benefits, who is hurt, and who is only concept exposure.
- Convert uncertainty into key variables and an evidence plan.
- End with a preliminary status: Discard, Watch, Deep dive, or Potential investment hypothesis.
- Always include kill conditions.

## Flexible workflow

Do not force every topic into every section. Use the framework adaptively:
- For simple questions, compress the value-chain work.
- For complex sector themes, expand the chain map and variable watchlist.
- For early narratives, emphasize evidence plan and disconfirmation.
- For mature companies, emphasize profit pool, margins, valuation risk, and ROIC.
