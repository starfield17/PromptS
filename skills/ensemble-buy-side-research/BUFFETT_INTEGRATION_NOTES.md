## Main Behavioral Change

The skill now treats business quality, moat durability, circle of competence, and permanent-impairment risk as judgment gates rather than soft commentary. A thesis outside the circle of competence, without durable moat evidence, or with ruin risk should be downgraded even if the narrative and near-term catalyst look attractive.
